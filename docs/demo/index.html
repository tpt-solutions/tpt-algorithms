tpt-algo in the browser

Two of the crates compiled to WebAssembly and run here, in your browser. Everything runs locally: no data leaves the page. Both demos use seeded synthetic data, so a given seed always gives the same result.

Anomaly scoring: Isolation Forest (tpt-algo-fraud)

A forest is trained on 400 normal points around the origin plus 12 outliers in the upper right. Each point is coloured by its anomaly score (blue = normal, orange = anomalous). Click the plot to score any point.

Battery state of charge: Extended Kalman filter (tpt-algo-soc)

A simulated cell starts at 90% state of charge and discharges at 1 A for 50 minutes. The filter is told it starts at 50%, so it has to converge from a wrong guess. Voltage readings carry noise. The first reading pulls the estimate up sharply, past the truth, and then it settles onto the true curve. The simulated cell has a slightly higher series resistance (0.06 Ω) than the filter assumes (0.05 Ω).
